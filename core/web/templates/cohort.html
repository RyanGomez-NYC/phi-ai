Cohort builder

Population counting over the OMOP layer, entirely on the de-identified plane. This is a separate surface rather than a wider filter on the assistant for an architectural reason: the attribution gate requires every cited chunk's subject to match the queried patient, so no generative answer over identified data can span patients. Cohort work routes through de-identification by construction, not by policy.
